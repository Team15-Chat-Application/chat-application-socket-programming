## Development, testing and contributions

- Follow the submitted SRS (`SE_SRS_Division_of_Work.pdf`), SAD (`15_SAD.pdf`)
  and Test Plan (`15_TestPlan.pdf`). Record and explain departures from submitted
  documents; retain requirement/test traceability.
- Implement the work allocated to each sprint, with relevant unit/integration tests
  and GitHub Actions checks for commits and PRs alongside development.
- The assigned team member must raise the PR. Preserve genuine authorship and
  contributions; do not attribute work to a teammate who did not contribute.
  GPT assistance is allowed by Part 2. Work performed for Soham can use his
  authorized identity. For work assigned to other members, prepare a handoff so
  they can raise their own PRs under their own identities.
- Repository owners review and commit/merge PRs. Preserve test-review and sign-off
  responsibilities from the Test Plan, including Yaswanth's QA Lead test-case
  review. Requested reviews and passing CI are not proof of human approval.
- A GitHub Project, an issue backlog and a milestone are different artifacts.
  Verify the required Project exists and is linked to the repository.
- Story points are estimates; keep proposed scope/estimates distinct from team
  agreement. Part 2 does not supply feature allocation or point values.

## Sprint outcomes

- Sprint 1: deliver a working product, record a one-minute outcome video and upload
  that video to the repository.
- Sprint 2: deliver a working product, record a two-minute outcome video, complete
  the documentation and freeze the development cycle.
- Document deviations and explain them during the final demo. Retain authentic
  execution, review and demo evidence; helper modules tested with fixtures are not
  automatically a working end-to-end product.
